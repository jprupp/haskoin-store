Output of `haskoin-store-bench import +RTS -N4 -A64m`, 2026-11-15.

AMD Ryzen 9 3900X, four cores given to the program, RocksDB 11.1.1, the
databases on tmpfs, a block cache of 32 MiB. "Sequential" is
importBlockSequential without the read cache; "in stages" is importBlock.

There are six sets of runs here, for the six tables of
docs/block-import.md. Every stage was faster in the later sets, made
later the same day, than in the first: compare within a set. The fifth
and the sixth set have four kinds of block, where the others have two.
